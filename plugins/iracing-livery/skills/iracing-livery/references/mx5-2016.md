# Global Mazda MX-5 Cup (MX-5 2016): a mapped template

These are measured on the post-rebuild 2048×2048 template (the car's "Mazda MX5 2016.psd").
The orientations were confirmed in the sim with screenshots, apart from the line marked
unconfirmed. Island ids are what `template_map.py` gives this template; in code, find them
by the sample point so a re-run can't shift them.

Paint folder: `Documents\iRacing\paint\mx5 mx52016\`.

Number blocks (the sim draws the number in these):
- left door: 494-617 × 1810-1915;
- right door: 494-617 × 577-682;
- rear bumper: 358-422 × 240-305.

A light panel of about 468-640 × 1790-1950 on the left door (mirrored for the right), with
the name starting after x 650, kept every number legible.

Places that worked:
- **Sides:** the name between x 650 and 1255, centred near y 1870; a dark sill band from
  y 1950 for the web address.
- **Hood:** a plate centred at (368, 1245), turned -90°, at most about 600 px long.
- **Trunk:** a plate centred at (1547, 394), upright, at most about 450 px wide so it clears
  the panel's edges.
- **Front bumper:** two small words centred at (1010, 1085) and (1690, 1085), each inside
  125 × 30. They sit above the intakes and clear of the stamped plate and emblem. Lower
  down, the intakes cut letters off.
- **Rear bumper:** the web address to the right of the number block, near (634, 262).
  *Unconfirmed:* text higher on the bumper (y ≈ 115, between the tail lamps) did not show
  in a high rear view and may sit under the trunk's overhang.

The template marks two small swatch squares, named for the pit box and cockpit colours:
"Pitbox Colors" at 2021-2045 and "Cockpit Color" at 277-309 × 2010-2042. Paint them on
purpose, in the brand's colours, rather than leaving whatever the ground happened to be.

What the sim stamps on this car (Car Mandatory): maker logos, the model badge, the plate on
the front bumper, black trim. Keep words and fine detail off those spots.
